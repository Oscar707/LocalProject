00 Goal

Understand the 369 method. Know what is true and what is myth. Have phrases that you can use today, and a tracker to do it.

01 What it is

The 369 method is a journaling ritual for "manifestation". You pick 1 short phrase (your intention). Then you write it by hand:

WhenTimesPurpose
Morning3Set the intention for the day.
Afternoon6Remind yourself in the middle of the day.
Night9Make it the last thought before sleep.
  • Total: 18 lines per day, about 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Most people do it for 21, 33 or 45 days in a row.
  • Keep the same phrase for all the days.
  • It became popular on TikTok around 2020 to 2021.

02 Is it from Nikola Tesla?

Short answer: no. Tesla did not make this method. People only use his name.

True

  • Tesla had strange habits with the number 3. His biographers say he walked around a building 3 times before he went in, and he liked hotel room numbers that 3 can divide.
  • These are signs of obsessive habits, not a spiritual system.

Not proven

  • The famous quote "If you only knew the magnificence of the 3, 6 and 9, you would have the key to the universe" has no known source. Nobody found it in his writings or interviews.
  • Tesla never wrote about manifestation or writing phrases 3, 6 and 9 times.

03 Does it work?

The universe does not respond to the number of times you write a phrase. There is no evidence for that part.

But some parts of the ritual can help you, for normal psychology reasons:

What helps

  • Clarity: you must decide what you want, in 1 sentence.
  • Attention: 3 times per day, you remember your goal. You notice chances to act.
  • Habit: 33 days of a small daily ritual builds discipline.
  • Calm: slow handwriting at night can relax you.

What can hurt

  • Only dreaming: research by Gabriele Oettingen shows that only positive fantasy can lower your effort. Your brain feels like you already got it.
  • Phrases you do not believe: a study (Wood et al., 2009) found that "I am a lovable person" made people with low self-esteem feel worse.
  • Outcome you cannot control: "I win $10 000" depends on luck. Results then feel like failure.
Use it as a focus and habit tool, not as magic. Systems over goals.

04 How to write a good phrase

RuleWeakStrong
Present tenseI will be fit.I train 4 times each week.
You control itI get rich.I save 20% of each paycheck.
BelievableI am the best player in the world.I get better with each game I review.
Short(3 lines of text)1 line, under 15 words.
Positive wordsI do not scroll at night.My phone stays outside the bedroom.

Tip: start with "I am", "I do", "I choose" or "I am becoming". "I am becoming" helps when the full "I am" feels false.

06 Upgrade: add a plan (WOOP)

Oettingen's research shows a better result when you add the obstacle and a plan. Use the night session (9 lines) for this:

  1. Wish: your 369 phrase.
  2. Outcome: the best result, in 1 line.
  3. Obstacle: the thing inside you that stops you.
  4. Plan: "If [obstacle], then I will [action]."
Example: "I am in bed by 11 pm." Obstacle: I watch videos in bed. Plan: If it is 10:45 pm, then I put my phone on the charger in the kitchen.

Write the phrase 8 times, then the if-then plan 1 time. This keeps the ritual and adds an action.
