00 Goal

End of the path: you build 2 small tools and explain each formula in your own words:

  • A WPA calculator for a League game: P(win) before and after each event, and who added the most win chance.
  • An EV + position-size calculator for a trading setup: expectancy per trade, risk of ruin, Kelly size.

Plan backward → build forward

01 EV→02 Variance→03 Sizing→04 Bayes→05 Win-prob model→06 WPA→07 Your edge

WPA needs a win-probability model. The model needs conditional probability. Knowing if an edge is real needs variance and Bayes. All of it starts with EV.
