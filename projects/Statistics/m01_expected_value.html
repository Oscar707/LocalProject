07 Module summary

Key formulas

EV = p₁·x₁ + p₂·x₂ + … (all p add up to 1) 2 outcomes: EV = p·W − (1−p)·L Break-even win rate = L / (W + L) = 1 / (1 + R) (R = W / L) Total expectancy = EV per trade × number of trades

Short reading (after the cards, not before)

  • Annie Duke, Thinking in Bets, chapter 1 (~20 min): good decisions vs good results.
  • Van K. Tharp, "expectancy" and "R-multiples" (search the 2 terms, read 1 short article): how traders measure EV per trade.
  • [Expected value, intro only](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expected_value) (~5 min).
