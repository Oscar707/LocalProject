Part 1 · Rounded shoulders

10 min
1

Thoracic extension

1.5 min
Foam roller or chair back · 3 spots × 5 reps
  • Put the roller under your upper back. Hands support your head.
  • Extend back over the roller. Keep your ribs down.
  • Do not arch your lower back.
  • Move the roller up 3 cm. Do again.

2

Doorway pec stretch

2.5 min
2 × 30 s each side
  • Forearm on the door frame. Elbow at shoulder height.
  • Step forward slowly until you feel the chest pull.
  • Keep shoulders down. Do not arch your back.
  • Set 2: put the elbow higher (upper chest).

3

Chin tucks

2 min
2 × 10 · hold 5 s
  • Sit tall or lie on your back.
  • Move your chin straight back. Make a "double chin".
  • Do not look down.
  • Feel the back of the neck get long.

4

Wall angels

2 min
2 × 10 slow
  • Back on the wall. Feet 30 cm out. Knees soft.
  • Lower back, upper back and head touch the wall.
  • Arms in "W". Slide up to "Y". Pull elbows down to your back pockets.
  • Stop where the arms come off the wall.
Video Images Guide
5

Prone Y-T raise

2 min
2 × (8 Y + 8 T) · or band pull-apart 2 × 15
  • Lie face down. Forehead on a towel. Thumbs up.
  • Lift arms in a "Y". Hold 2 s. Then do the "T".
  • Pull shoulder blades down and back.
  • Do not shrug.

Part 2 · Anterior pelvic tilt

10 min
6

Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch

3.5 min
2 × 45 s each side
  • Kneel on one knee. Put a pad under it.
  • First tuck the pelvis under ("tail under"). Squeeze the back-leg glute.
  • Then shift forward 2–5 cm only.
  • Ribs down. If your back arches, you lose the stretch.
  • Option: arm up on the stretch side.
Video Images Guide
7

Glute bridge

2.5 min
2 × 12 · hold 2 s at top
  • Lie on your back. Knees bent. Feet hip width.
  • First flatten your lower back to the floor.
  • Push through the heels. Lift the hips. Squeeze the glutes.
  • Stop when the body is a straight line. Do not arch.
Video Images
8

Dead bug

2.5 min
2 × 8 each side · slow
  • Lie on your back. Knees at 90°. Arms up.
  • Press the lower back into the floor. Keep it there.
  • Extend the opposite arm and leg. Breathe out.
  • If the back lifts, make the movement smaller.

Child's pose

1.5 min
2 × 40 s
  • Knees wide. Hips back to the heels.
  • Arms long in front.
  • Breathe into the lower back. Let it round.

Part 3 · Squat balance

10 min

Falling back = usually stiff ankles (calves). Fix: calf length + ankle bend + weight in front.

10

Calf stretch, 2 ways

2 min
Each side: 30 s straight knee + 30 s bent knee
  • Hands on the wall. Back foot points forward.
  • Back heel stays down.
  • Straight back knee = upper calf.
  • Bent back knee = lower calf (soleus). Soleus matters most for squats.
Video Images Guide
11

Knee-to-wall rocks

2 min
2 × 10 each side
  • Half-kneel, face a wall. Front toes 5–10 cm from the wall.
  • Push the knee forward over the 2nd–3rd toe to touch the wall.
  • Heel stays down. Arch does not collapse.
  • When it is easy, move the foot back 1 cm.
Video Images Guide
12

Adductor rockback

2 min
2 × 8 each side
  • Start on hands and knees.
  • Put one leg straight out to the side. Foot flat.
  • Push the hips back toward the heels. Back stays flat.
  • Pinch on the outer hip? Make the leg less wide.
Video Images Guide
13

Supported deep squat hold

2 min
3 × 30 s · hold a door frame or table
  • Feet shoulder width. Toes out 15–30°.
  • Hold the support. Sit down between the heels.
  • Push knees out over the toes. Heels down. Chest up.
  • Each week, use the hands less.
Video Images
14

Goblet squat, heels raised

2 min
2 × 8 · 3 s down · 1 s pause
  • Hold a weight at the chest (dumbbell, bag, water jug).
  • The weight in front keeps you from falling back.
  • Heels on a book or plate, 2–3 cm high.
  • Sit down slowly. Pause. Stand up.
  • Lower the heels over the weeks.

In bed · phone and movies

Short answer: the number of pillows is not the problem. A bent neck (chin to chest) and arms that hold the phone up are the problem. One pillow is OK. Many pillows under the head only is the worst.

Avoid: pillows under the head only

Avoid
  • The pillows push the head forward. The upper back stays flat.
  • This is the same shape as rounded shoulders and forward head.

Best: recline on a ramp

Best
  • Make a ramp: 2–3 pillows from the shoulder blades up, or a wedge pillow. About 30–45°.
  • Head stays in line with the back. Chin does not drop.
  • Bend the knees. Put the phone on the knees or on a pillow on the lap.
  • Phone at eye level. Arms rest on the bed or belly.

OK: side lying

OK
  • 1 pillow, thick enough to fill the gap between the shoulder and the head.
  • Hug a pillow. It stops the top shoulder from rolling forward.
  • Phone on a stand or against a pillow. Do not hold it.
  • Change sides at half time.
  • Change position each 20–30 min. A movie = 3 to 4 changes.
  • After the movie: 10 chin tucks (card 3) + 30 s doorway stretch each side (card 2).
