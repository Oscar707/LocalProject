Q Questions first

1. Which units do we list?

List the units that are vacant now, and the units where the tenant has said they will leave at the end of the lease. 12 units at about 80% occupancy = about 2–3 vacant units.

  • Fill in the unit table below. The Action column tells you what to do with each unit.
  • Most Quebec leases end June 30. Tenants usually send their "I will not renew" notice 3 to 6 months before. Units leaving July 1 can go on the list in the spring.
  • Students look for housing hardest for January (now) and for July 1 / September.

2. Where do we get photos?

Do not copy the Centris / Realtor.ca photos. The photographer or the broker owns them. The OACIQ (Quebec broker regulator) says you need the photographer's written permission to use them again.

  • Best, free Take your own phone photos of the vacant units in daylight. Use the shot list in Card 04.
  • Ask Ask Kai Yuan for the photos and a written OK (an email is enough) from the photographer.
  • Optional: hire a photographer once, and get a contract that says you can use the photos anywhere.

3. Can we post now, or wait for the Kai Yuan contract to end?

It depends on the contract you signed. Find it and read 3 things: the end date, whether it says "exclusive", and the clause that says when commission is owed.

  • Not in the contract The units Kai Yuan does not list: you can post them now.
  • Exclusive contract You can usually post too, but the broker can still get the commission if you sign a lease during the contract. The OACIQ residential lease form also keeps commission owed if you sign, in the 180 days after the end, with a person who was interested during the contract.
  • Easy fix Ask Kai Yuan to amend the contract so you can also market the units (the OACIQ explains how to make it non-exclusive). Get it in writing.
  • I am not a lawyer. Free OACIQ info line: 1-800-440-7170.

4. Can I make accounts and prep now?

Yes Accounts, drafts, photos and texts are prep, not advertising. Do everything below now. Only publish a unit under the Kai Yuan contract after question 3 is clear.

Claude cannot make the accounts for you: each site checks your own email and phone. Each one takes about 5 minutes.

04 Photos & media

Students skip listings with few photos. Shoot in daylight, landscape, lights on. Order: living room → kitchen → bedrooms → bathroom → building front → street / metro.

  • Living room (2 angles)
  • Kitchen + appliances
  • Each bedroom
  • Each bathroom
  • Basement bedroom with window (if any)
  • Building front
  • Balcony / yard
  • Floor plan (sketch is OK)
  • Short video walk-through (optional)

05 Quebec rules check

Check each line before you post. Students read these rules in the McGill renting guide, so a listing that follows them looks more trustworthy.

  • No deposit asked. A deposit is illegal in Quebec. First month rent at lease signing is normal.
  • The application does not ask for SIN, credit card, passport number, or citizenship.
  • No wording that excludes people by origin, sex, religion, age, student or international status, etc.
  • Use the mandatory TAL lease form (Tribunal administratif du logement).
  • Building built in the last 5 years: fill in Section F of the lease (rent-increase exemption). Confirm the details with the TAL.
  • If a broker has a brokerage contract on the unit: get the broker's OK before you post it yourself.
  • Full street address in the listing. Students treat listings without an address as possible scams.
  • Real photos of this unit only, not stock or model photos.
  • A co-signer (guarantor) request is OK. Say so in the listing if you need one.
